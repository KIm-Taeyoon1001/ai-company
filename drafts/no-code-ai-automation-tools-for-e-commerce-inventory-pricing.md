### Implementation Checklist for Non‑Technical Merchants
1. **Define Goals** – inventory turnover, margin targets, or order fulfillment speed.
2. **Select a Unified Platform** – choose one that bundles forecasting, pricing, and routing.
3. **Connect Data Sources** – link your store, ERP, and carrier APIs via the platform’s connectors.
4. **Set Pricing Rules** – use the visual rule editor to set thresholds (e.g., increase price when inventory < 10 units).
5. **Run a Pilot** – test on a small product line for 2‑4 weeks.
6. **Measure ROI** – track stock‑out reduction, margin lift, and time saved (Digital Applied reports 9‑month payback).
7. **Scale Gradually** – expand to all SKUs once the pilot proves successful.
